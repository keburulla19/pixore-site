Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

The short version
  • Pixore keeps only what the game needs to work and to stay safe: your account, your progress and some security records.
  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics companies. We never sell or share your data for marketing.
  • We don't ask for your email, real name, phone number or payment details.
  • You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Who we are

Pixore is a free online game made by an independent developer in Finland ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the data described here. To contact us about privacy, see Contact below.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, ad networks, tracking cookies or analytics tools, in the game or on this website.
  • No selling, renting or trading of your data.
  • No collecting of email addresses, real names, phone numbers, contacts, photos, precise location or payment details.

Who else handles data

  • UpCloud rents us the server the game runs on (it stores the data above for us).
  • GitHub hosts this website and the game downloads. Like any web host it sees your IP address when you visit.
  • Discord: if you join our Discord server, Discord's own privacy policy applies there. Bug and player reports are posted to a private staff channel there. On Windows, if Discord is open on your computer, the game shows "Playing Pixore" on your Discord profile; this happens on your own device through the Discord app, and you can turn it off in Discord's Activity Privacy settings.
  • itch.io: if you download the game there, itch.io's privacy policy applies to that.

Other players

Your username, character, name tag flag, chat messages and the worlds you build are visible to other players. Online friends can see which world you are in and warp to you, unless you turn off "Friends can warp to me" in the Friends window.

Children

Pixore is meant for players aged 13 and up. If you are younger, please ask a parent or guardian before playing. Don't share personal information (your real name, address, school, phone number or passwords) with anyone in the game. If you are a parent and want your child's account removed, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we have about you, correct it, or delete it. If you live in the EU / EEA you also have the right to object to how we use your data and to complain to a data protection authority (in Finland: the Data Protection Ombudsman, tietosuoja.fi). We use your data to provide the game you signed up for, and to keep it safe and working.

Deleting your account

Ask us through any contact below with your username. To prove the account is yours, also tell us something only you would know (for example an item in your inventory or a world you own), or ask from inside the game. We delete your account and progress, the worlds you ask us to remove, and your entry in the account-per-IP list, usually within 7 days. Copies may remain in backups, which are only used to recover from failures; a deleted account is never restored.
