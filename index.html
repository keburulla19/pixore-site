Welcome to Pixore

Pixore is a cozy sandbox MMO made one pixel at a time. Claim your own worlds and build anything, farm and crossbreed, fish the ponds, mine for gems and hang out with friends in the floating HUB.

Put down a World Lock and the world is yours: only you and the friends you give rights can build there.

Phone and PC players share the same worlds, with one account on both.

Farm & crossbreed

Every block has a seed. Plant it on dirt and it grows into a tree full of blocks, seeds and gems. Plant a different seed on a growing tree to crossbreed something brand new, and fill your Recipe Book.

Grow carrots, pumpkins and starfruit, then cook them into dishes at the Oven.

Fish the ponds

Cast your lure, strike when it bites and win the reel fight. 14 species in 5 sizes, rods and lures to upgrade, and the Fishmonger pays for your catch.

Dig into the Mines

Take a pickaxe down through five mine levels for gems, nuggets and rare stones. Haul your loot out before the monsters get you.

Trade & Market

Trade face to face with other players, or use Pixel Bits to buy and sell on the Market. Get Pixel Bits at the Pixel Exchange in the HUB, or place a Market Terminal in your own world.
